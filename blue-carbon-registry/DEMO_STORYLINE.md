## 🎯 Judge Q&A Preparation

### Expected Questions & Answers

**Q: "How do you ensure photo authenticity?"**
A: "Multi-layer approach: EXIF metadata analysis, Error Level Analysis for tampering detection, perceptual hashing for duplicates, and GPS coordinate validation."

**Q: "What about scalability to thousands of projects?"**
A: "Microservices architecture with Docker, Redis caching, database optimization, and Polygon's high throughput blockchain."

**Q: "How do you handle offline scenarios?"**
A: "SQLite local storage with Drift ORM, background sync with WorkManager, conflict resolution, and offline-first state management."

**Q: "What's your go-to-market strategy?"**
A: "Partner with existing NGO networks, government pilot programs, integrate with carbon marketplaces, and freemium model for small NGOs."

**Q: "How is this different from existing solutions?"**
A: "First mobile-first MRV system, automated AI verification, real satellite integration, blockchain transparency, and specifically designed for blue carbon projects."
